Model used
Before selection: AA = p², Aa = 2pq, aa = q². After viability selection,
p′ = [p²wAA + pqwAa] / w̄
w̄ = p²wAA + 2pqwAa + q²waa

The new allele frequency becomes the starting frequency of the next generation. The model assumes an infinite population and is deterministic: no genetic drift, mutation, migration, assortative mating, or frequency-dependent fitness.
